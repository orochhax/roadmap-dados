# Simulado autoral — EF SET de quatro habilidades

Este treino verifica reading, listening, writing e speaking em contexto de dados. Não reproduz itens do EF SET e não prevê a pontuação oficial.

## Preparação do ambiente

- Tempo total: 80 minutos.
- Use computador, fone, microfone e local silencioso.
- Não use tradutor, legenda, dicionário, IA ou corretor gramatical.
- Grave as respostas em `respostas-ef-set.md` e os áudios em arquivos separados.
- Antes da tentativa, peça a outra pessoa ou ao assistente para abrir [roteiros-listening-ef-set.md](roteiros-listening-ef-set.md) e reproduzir os áudios sem mostrar o texto. O candidato não deve abrir esse arquivo antes de responder.
- Não há respostas nem gabarito; envie texto e áudio para correção.

## Parte 1 — reading — 20 minutos

Leia o texto uma vez e responda em inglês.

### Text

A customer-retention team built a model to identify subscribers who might cancel their contracts. The first version achieved high accuracy, so the team planned to contact every customer classified as high risk. During error analysis, however, an analyst found that only eight percent of customers actually cancelled. A model that predicted “no cancellation” for everyone would therefore look accurate while producing no useful leads.

The team replaced accuracy as its main decision metric and compared precision and recall at several thresholds. A lower threshold found more future cancellations, but it also increased the number of customers contacted unnecessarily. This mattered because each call had a real operational cost and the retention team could contact only five thousand people per week. The analysts selected a threshold under that capacity constraint and reported both the expected benefit and the uncertainty around it.

Before deployment, they also checked results by contract type, region and tenure. Performance was weaker for new customers in two regions. Rather than hiding the issue in the overall average, the team documented the limitation and proposed a monitored pilot. The final recommendation was less exciting than the first presentation, but it was more honest and easier for the business to test.

### Questions

1. What made accuracy misleading in this case?
2. How did the contact limit affect the technical decision?
3. Explain the trade-off created by lowering the threshold.
4. Why did the team inspect results by subgroup?
5. What does the passage suggest about a useful technical recommendation?
6. Give a title of no more than eight words for the passage.
7. Replace `hiding the issue` with another expression that preserves the meaning.
8. Write one question the retention manager should ask before approving the pilot.

## Parte 2 — listening — 15 minutos

Não leia os roteiros do final. Peça para reproduzirem cada áudio uma única vez em velocidade natural. Depois responda em inglês.

### Audio 1 questions

1. What problem delayed the report?
2. Which two checks did the speaker request?
3. When is the revised result expected?
4. What should not be done before the checks are complete?

### Audio 2 questions

5. What are the two approaches being compared?
6. Why might the more accurate approach still be rejected?
7. Which measurements are required in the next experiment?
8. What decision will the measurements support?

## Parte 3 — writing — 25 minutos

Escreva em inglês sem corretor externo.

### Task A — 90 a 120 palavras

Write an email to a manager explaining that a dashboard number changed after you corrected duplicated records. Include:

- what happened;
- what was corrected;
- how you validated the new number;
- what limitation remains;
- what the manager should do next.

### Task B — 150 a 200 palavras

Your team tested an embedding-based matching system and a lower-cost rule-based baseline. The embedding system improved recall but increased latency and cost. Write a recommendation that compares the alternatives, states what evidence is still missing and proposes the next experiment.

## Parte 4 — speaking — 20 minutos

Grave uma resposta por arquivo. Não leia texto pronto.

1. **60 seconds:** Introduce yourself and explain why you are studying data science.
2. **90 seconds:** Explain one roadmap project to a non-technical manager: problem, data, method, result and limitation.
3. **90 seconds:** A stakeholder asks you to publish a model because its accuracy is high. Respond with the checks you need first.
4. **120 seconds:** Compare a simple baseline and a complex AI solution in terms of quality, cost, latency and maintenance.
5. **60 seconds:** Describe a mistake you found in an analysis and how you corrected it.

## Rubrica de correção — sem respostas

| Habilidade | Critérios | Pontos |
|---|---|---:|
| Reading | ideia central, detalhe, inferência, vocabulário em contexto | 25 |
| Listening | ideia central, detalhe, número/prazo, intenção do falante | 25 |
| Writing | clareza, organização, gramática, vocabulário e cumprimento da tarefa | 25 |
| Speaking | clareza, fluidez, pronúncia inteligível, vocabulário e resposta ao cenário | 25 |
| **Total** |  | **100** |

Uma resposta sofisticada que não atende ao pedido perde pontos. Erros pequenos contam menos que uma mensagem impossível de entender. A correção deve registrar exemplos, padrões de erro e uma ação de estudo para cada habilidade.

### Critério de prontidão

- 70% ou mais em cada habilidade;
- nenhuma ajuda externa durante a tentativa;
- áudio e microfone funcionando;
- capacidade de concluir dentro do tempo.

O resultado deste simulado não autoriza informar um nível CEFR. Somente o teste oficial emite a pontuação que poderá ser registrada.
