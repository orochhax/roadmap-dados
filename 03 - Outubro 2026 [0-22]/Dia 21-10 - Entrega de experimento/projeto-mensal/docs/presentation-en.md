# Experiment Presentation in English

> Write a spoken script in English and rehearse it with a timer. The final delivery must last 2–3 minutes. Replace every prompt with your own project evidence.

## Opening — 15–20 seconds

<!-- Introduce the decision, the audience, and why the experiment matters. -->

## Problem and Data — 25–35 seconds

<!-- Explain the hypothesis, primary outcome, groups, sample, and one relevant data-quality check. -->

## Method — 30–40 seconds

<!-- Explain the comparison, decision rule, uncertainty estimate, and sensitivity analysis in plain English. -->

## Results — 30–40 seconds

<!-- State the measured effect and interval, then explain the decision without overstating causality or certainty. -->

## Limitations — 20–30 seconds

<!-- Describe the most important limitation and what evidence would reduce that uncertainty. -->

## Reproduction and Closing — 20–30 seconds

<!-- Give the reproduction path or command, summarize the recommended next action, and invite questions. -->
